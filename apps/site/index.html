@markwylde/pet · v0.1

A tiny friend
for your website.

Little animated desktop pets in the spirit of Microsoft Agent. They idle, blink, fly when you drag them, nap when you ignore them, and chat with whatever AI you plug in. Zero dependencies.

Get started

psst, the little guy is real. Drag him. Throw him. Click him.

What's in the box

Small library. Big personality.

🪶

Zero dependencies

Plain DOM and SVG. Drop it into React, Vue, Svelte, htmx or a hand-written HTML page. There are optional React bindings if that's your thing.

🚁

Drag, throw, fly

Drag slowly and his propeller comes out. Drag fast and he goes turbo. Let go and he glides, bouncing off the edges of the screen.

💬

Chats with any AI

Hand him an assistant function and he'll think, then talk while the reply streams in. Bring your own backend.

⏳

Awaitable everything

await pet.moveTo('center'), await pet.play('happy'), await pet.say('hi'). Script little scenes, and they stop cleanly when someone grabs him.

😴

Naps in a dock

Leave him alone and he dives into a dock of your choice, like a tray icon. One click brings him bouncing back.

♿

Accessible & typed

Keyboard-operable (arrow keys fly him), labelled for screen readers, follows reduced motion. TypeScript with docs on every option.

Meet the crew

Pick a pal. Or build your own.

Toasty isn't in the library. He's made with defineCharacter() in about 40 lines on this page.

17 animations

Every mood, beautifully blended.

Hover a card to preview it, or click to make the real one do it. Animations are pure functions of time, so they blend smoothly into each other, and you can add your own.
